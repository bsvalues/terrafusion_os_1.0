Appraiser opens tool → runs report → reads numbers → decides what to investigate → opens another screen → manually identifies problem → figures out fix → applies it → manually writes memo
Hours of work. High error risk. Memo often skipped.
✓ AI-Native Way — Appraiser-Centric
Appraiser opens tool → AI has already run full diagnostic stack, identified all issues, classified each as data/rate/outlier/external, proposed specific fixes, drafted the Calibration Memo → Appraiser reviews, overrides anything they disagree with, signs
Minutes of expert review. Full audit trail. Memo is complete.
What the AI Does Automatically (Before You Touch Anything)

Runs full PRD/PRB/COD/COV on every cell. Flags every deviation. Ranks severity. Classifies problem type for each finding.
② CLASSIFIES EACH ISSUE

For Data Problems: which specific records to investigate
For External Factors: recommended sales window shift

The Critical Question — Mass Adjustment vs. Data Problem
When AI flags Reval 6 at -15.5%, the appraiser needs to know: is this a rate problem (base rate is wrong for Historic Richland) or a data problem (the ratio study has outlier sales from historic renovations skewing the factor)?
RATE PROBLEM
All Historic Richland parcels are systematically over/under assessed → adjust the area factor
Fix: Recalibrate R6 factor from 0.845 → X
DATA PROBLEM
2-3 outlier sales in Historic Richland (renovated Victorians?) are skewing the ratio study → remove them
Fix: Flag parcels, re-run ratio study without outliers
EXTERNAL FACTOR
New construction boom / market shift in Historic Richland in 2024 makes old sales window invalid
Fix: Shift sales window, weight recent sales heavier
The AI-native workbench tells you WHICH one this is — and gives you the data to verify it. The appraiser makes the final call.
