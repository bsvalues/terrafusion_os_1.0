⚠ Regressivity Pattern Confirmed

The ratio gradient from Q1 (1.18) to Q5 (1.00) is a textbook regressivity pattern. Low-value homeowners bear a disproportionate tax burden. IAAO PRB of −0.031 quantifies this: for every $100k increase in value, the assessment ratio drops ~3.1 percentage points.

⚠ Age + Value Interaction

Pre-1960 properties in Q1 show a median ratio of 1.21 — the worst stratum in the county. These are older, lower-value homes that haven't been physically inspected recently. Reval physical inspection should prioritize this cohort.

✓ New Construction is Equitable

Post-2015 construction across all quintiles sits within IAAO range (0.95–1.02). New homes are being assessed correctly. The problem is concentrated in pre-1980 stock — targeted adjustment + reval inspection will resolve it without touching the clean strata.
