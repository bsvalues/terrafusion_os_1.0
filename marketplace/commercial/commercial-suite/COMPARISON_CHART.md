## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I switch between editions?**
A: Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Data and settings are preserved.

**Q: Is the software different between editions?**
A: No, the core platform is identical. Only data access and support levels differ.

**Q: Can commercial and government editions share data?**
A: Yes, government can share public data with commercial users through the data marketplace.

**Q: Do I need different training for different editions?**
A: No, the interface and features are the same. Government gets additional compliance training.

**Q: Can I try before buying?**
A: Commercial has 30-day free trial. Government can request a pilot program. Enterprise can get a POC.
