# LEV-111 - DOR Report Types and Certification Process

## Overview

The Washington Department of Revenue (DOR) requires county assessors to
submit several reports during the annual levy certification cycle.

## Report Types

### 1. Levy Certification (Form REV 64 0100)

- Submitted after all district levies are set.
- Contains each district's certified levy amount and rate.
- Due date: typically late January for the following tax year.

### 2. Abstract of Assessed Valuations

- Summarizes total AV by district and property class.
- Used by DOR for state-level tax analysis.
- Broken into real property, personal property, and utilities.

### 3. Tax Rate Computation Worksheet

- Shows how each district's rate was calculated.
- Includes HLL comparison, statutory limit checks, and pro-ration.

### 4. Levy Balance Sheet

- Reconciles requested levies against certified amounts.
- Identifies districts that were reduced due to statutory limits.

## Certification Timeline

| Step | Date Range     | Action                                    |
|------|----------------|-------------------------------------------|
| 1    | Oct 1-15       | Preliminary AV values set                 |
| 2    | Nov 1-30       | Districts submit levy requests            |
| 3    | Dec 1-15       | Assessor computes rates and HLL           |
| 4    | Dec 15-31      | Pro-ration applied if needed              |
| 5    | Jan 1-15       | Final certification to DOR                |

## Submission Format

Reports are submitted via the DOR online portal or as signed PDF/XLSX files.
