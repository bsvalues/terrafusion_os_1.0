# LEV-134 - Certification Documents

## Overview

Describes the certification document package produced by BCBSLevy at the
conclusion of the annual levy cycle. These documents constitute the
official record of levy rates and amounts for the tax year.

## Document Package Contents

### 1. Certification Resolution

- Official statement signed by the county assessor.
- Lists each taxing district with its certified levy and rate.
- Includes the assessor's attestation of accuracy.

### 2. Rate Computation Worksheets

- One worksheet per taxing district.
- Shows HLL calculation, requested vs. certified amounts.
- Includes statutory limit checks and any pro-ration applied.

### 3. Aggregate Rate Summary

- Lists every TCA with its aggregate levy rate.
- Confirms no TCA exceeds the constitutional 1% limit.
- Shows senior/junior breakdown for pro-rated TCAs.

### 4. Compliance Checklist

- Signed attestation that all statutory requirements were met.
- Lists any compliance flags raised and their resolution.
- References applicable RCW and WAC sections.

## Retention

Certification documents are retained for a minimum of 6 years per
state retention schedules (Local Government Common Records Retention
Schedule, GS2010-065).
