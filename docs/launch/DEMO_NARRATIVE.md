# Demo Script: "The Monday Morning Appeal"
**Presenter:** Bill
**Duration:** 5 Minutes
**Goal:** Show the Assessor how their life gets easier.

---

### [0:00] The Setting
**BILL:** "Imagine it's Monday morning. You have a stack of 50 appeals on your desk. Usually, this means days of digging through maps, spreadsheets, and old MLS listings. Let's see how we do it today."

*(Open TerraFusion Dashboard)*

### [0:45] The Search
**BILL:** "I have Parcel 112233 on the docket. The taxpayer says their value is too high because 'the house down the street sold for less'. Let's check."

*(Type 'Parcel 112233' into Search)*

**BILL:** "Instantly, I see the Subject Property. But here's the magic. I click **'Valuation Analysis'**."

### [1:30] The Comparative Analysis
*(Show Comps Grid)*

**BILL:** "The system has already found the best 5 sales in the neighborhood. But look here—" *(Point to a Red Flag)* "—it highlighted the sale the taxpayer mentioned. It says 'Distressed Sale - Foreclosure'. The AI knows that's not a valid comparison."

### [2:30] The Refinement
**BILL:** "I can uncheck that invalid sale. Watch the value recalibrate instantly."

*(Uncheck box. Value updates)*

**BILL:** "Now I have 4 tough, defensible sales. I'm confident in this number."

### [3:30] The Report
**BILL:** "Finally, I need to present this to the Board. I don't want to type a 10-page report. I click **'Generate Defense Package'**."

*(Click Button. PDF loads)*

**BILL:** "Map. Grid. Adjustments. Photos. Done. I just finished a 4-hour job in 4 minutes."

### [4:30] Closing
**BILL:** "This isn't about replacing appraisers. It's about letting appraisers be appraisers, instead of data entry clerks. That is TerraFusion."

*(End Demo)*
