# TerraFusion OS: Stakeholder Briefing (v1.1 Sovereign)

## Slide 1: Title
**TerraFusion OS: The Future of County Assessment**
*Subtitle: Efficiency. Accuracy. Sovereignty.*

## Slide 2: The Challenge
*   **Rising Workloads:** Appeal volumes are increasing +20% year-over-year.
*   **Static Resources:** Staff counts remain flat.
*   **Data Complexity:** Systems (PACS, GIS, Permits) don't talk to each other proficiently.

## Slide 3: The Solution - TerraFusion OS
*   It is not just "software." It is a **Unified Operating System**.
*   It connects your existing PACS data with superior analytical capability.
*   It respects your boundaries: Built specifically for Washington State assessment law.

## Slide 4: Core Pillar - Sovereignty
*   **Your Data, Your House:** Unlike cloud SaaS, TerraFusion runs on **your** servers.
*   **No Leaks:** Sensitive taxpayer data does not leave the firewall.
*   **Control:** You decide when updates happen, not a vendor.

## Slide 5: Core Pillar - Efficiency
*   **Case Study: Residential Appeals**
    *   *Old Way:* 4 hours gathering comps, printed maps, manual adjustments.
    *   *TerraFusion Way:* 15 minutes. AI pre-selects valid comps, flags outliers, and drafts the defense report for your review.

## Slide 6: Core Pillar - Accuracy
*   **Automated Quality Control:** The system scans 100% of properties every night.
*   **Anomaly Detection:** "Why is this 2,000 sqft home valued at $50k?" (Flags data entry errors instantly).
*   **Fairness:** Ensure consistent valuation models across all neighborhoods.
