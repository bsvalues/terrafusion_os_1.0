# TerraFusion OS: Quick Start Guide

**Welcome, Assessor.**
TerraFusion is your digital analyst, designed to handle the drudgery so you can make the decisions.

---

## 🔐 1. How to Login
1.  Open Chrome/Edge and go to: `http://jcharrispacs` (or your internal IP).
2.  **Username:** Your domain login.
3.  **Password:** Your domain password.

---

## 🔍 2. The "Golden Query"
The Search Bar is your command center. You don't need code; just ask.

**Try these:**
*   *"Show me all single-family sales in District 4 above $500k from last month."*
*   *"Find outliers in Neighborhood 12 where Condition is 'Good' but Value is under $200k."*
*   *"Draft an appeal defense for Parcel 123-456 using the top 3 comps."*

---

## ⚠️ 3. The "Human Override"
TerraFusion is smart, but **YOU are the Authority.**

*   **If the AI is wrong:** Click the flag icon 🚩 next to the value.
*   **Correct it:** Enter the correct judgment (e.g., "Exclude comparable sale - non-arms-length transaction").
*   **The System Learns:** TerraFusion will remember your decision for next time.

---

## 🆘 Help & Support
*   **System Down?** Call IT.
*   **Data looks wrong?** Flag it in the system.
*   **Feature Idea?** click "Feedback" in the top right.
