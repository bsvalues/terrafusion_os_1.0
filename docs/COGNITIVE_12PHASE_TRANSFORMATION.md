# TerraFusion OS 12-Phase Transformation Framework

## 🌌 **TIER 4: ORGANIZATIONAL TRANSFORMATION**

### **When to Use 12 Phases**
> "Not harder 9—a different category entirely."

**Critical Distinction:**
```
3-6-9 Framework: Technical Execution
"We build the thing"

12 Framework: Organizational Transformation  
"We change how people work"
```

---

## 🎯 **12-Phase Decision Criteria**

### **Use TIER 4 (12 phases) when:**
- ✅ **100+ people** need to change behavior
- ✅ **Multiple departments/agencies** involved
- ✅ **New processes** replace existing workflows
- ✅ **Training programs** required at scale
- ✅ **Cultural resistance** expected
- ✅ **Executive sponsorship** and governance critical
- ✅ **Timeline** measured in quarters/years
- ✅ **Political navigation** required

### **DON'T use TIER 4 when:**
- ❌ Just building technical solution
- ❌ One team can execute independently  
- ❌ No process or behavior change required
- ❌ Timeline is weeks or months
- ❌ Tempted to use because "this is important"

### **The Litmus Test:**
> If you removed all technology and just focused on getting people to work differently, would you still have a massive project? **If YES → TIER 4**
