# Phase 12: County Launch Pack Specification

## Objective
Equip the TerraFusion field team with the communication artifacts necessary to validate the system with non-technical stakeholders (Assessors, County Leadership) and ensure adoption.

## Strategy
Shift focus from "Engineering Excellence" (how it works) to "Assessor Value" (what it does for them). Usage of technical jargon is strictly forbidden in these documents.

## Deliverables

### 1. Stakeholder Briefing (`docs/launch/STAKEHOLDER_DECK.md`)
*   **Target Audience:** County Commissioners, Assessor Leadership.
*   **Format:** Slide-by-slide Markdown outline.
*   **Key Themes:**
    *   **Sovereignty:** Your data never leaves your building (unless you want it to).
    *   **Efficiency:** Reduce appeal prep time from 4 hours to 15 minutes.
    *   **Accuracy:** "Measure Twice, Cut Once" using AI cross-validation.

### 2. Assessor "Cheat Sheet" (`docs/launch/ASSESSOR_ONE_PAGER.md`)
*   **Target Audience:** Daily users (Appraisers, Deputies).
*   **Format:** Single page, printable instructions.
*   **Content:**
    *   How to Login.
    *   The "Golden Query" (How to phrase questions).
    *   What to do if the AI is wrong (Override Protocol).

### 3. Security Posture Statement (`docs/launch/SECURITY_POSTURE.md`)
*   **Target Audience:** IT Directors, Auditors.
*   **Format:** Q&A / Fact Sheet.
*   **Key Defenses:**
    *   Air-gapped by default design.
    *   Encryption at Rest (Host MSSQL).
    *   Role-Based Zero-Trust access.

### 4. The Demo Narrative (`docs/launch/DEMO_NARRATIVE.md`)
*   **Target Audience:** The Presenter (Bill).
*   **Format:** Script / Storyboard.
*   **Scenario:** Managing a complex residential appeal with comparable sales data.
