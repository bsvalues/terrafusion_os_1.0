## 🤝 **ALTERNATIVE APPROACHES**

### **Option 1: Technical Co-Founder**
- **Find 1 senior engineer** willing to be co-founder
- **Give 20-25% equity** instead of salary
- **They help recruit** the next 2-3 engineers
- **Reduces your cash needs** to almost zero
- **Shared risk/reward** model

### **Option 2: Deferred Compensation**
- **Pay minimum viable salaries** ($40K-60K)
- **Defer additional $40K-60K** until funded
- **Higher equity percentages** (3-5% each)
- **Revenue sharing** once profitable
- **Guaranteed catch-up** when funded

### **Option 3: Revenue Sharing Model**
- **Lower base salaries** ($50K-70K)
- **10-15% of gross revenue** split among team
- **Equity on top** (2-3% each)
- **Scales with success** automatically
- **Aligns incentives** perfectly

## 🎯 **RECOMMENDED NEXT STEPS**

### **Before You Recruit:**
1. **Calculate your runway** - can you afford $25K/month for 12 months?
2. **Get legal docs ready** - equity agreements, employment contracts
3. **Prepare the demo** - show working product, not just slides
4. **Define milestones** - when salaries increase, when equity vests
5. **Have backup plan** - what if first 2 people say no?

### **During Recruitment:**
1. **Be completely honest** about compensation reality
2. **Show the upside potential** with comparable company exits
3. **Demonstrate product-market fit** with your county results
4. **Explain your unique advantages** in the government market
5. **Give them time to think** - this is a big decision

### **After They Say Yes:**
1. **Get everything in writing** immediately
2. **Set clear expectations** about equity vesting, salary increases
3. **Regular check-ins** about compensation satisfaction
4. **Celebrate milestones** that move toward better compensation
5. **Plan Series A fundraising** to get everyone to market salaries
