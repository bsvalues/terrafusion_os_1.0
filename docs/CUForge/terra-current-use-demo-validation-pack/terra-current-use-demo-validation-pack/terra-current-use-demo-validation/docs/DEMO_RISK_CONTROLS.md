# Demo Risk Controls

## Calculation Risk

Say: “This is a calculation engine scaffold with explainable ledger lines. Final production use requires county validation of levy rates, statutory interest, and policy configuration.”

## Notice Risk

Say: “Notice preview and controlled issuance are separated. Nothing is issued without human approval.”

## AI Risk

Say: “AI is disabled for first alpha or explain-only. It cannot approve, deny, waive, or issue.”

## Legal Risk

Say: “The system supports statutory workflow. It does not make legal determinations.”

## Data Risk

Say: “Demo data is synthetic unless explicitly connected to county data.”
