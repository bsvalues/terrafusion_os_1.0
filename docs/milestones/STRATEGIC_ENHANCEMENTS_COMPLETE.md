### **What Worked**:
1. **Starting with Documentation** - Made everything else easier
2. **One Week at a Time** - Focus prevented scope creep
3. **Testing Everything** - Caught issues immediately
4. **Beautiful UI** - Made tools people want to use
5. **THE TERRAFUSION WAY** - Principles guided every decision
