## Structural Correction

Before this Work Order, root governance allowed an authorized operator to merge, but the canonical
merge model still defaulted to Mode A outside individually bounded grants. That gap recreated routine
owner routing after each bounded envelope expired.

This Work Order separates two concerns:

1. Program and Work Order authority define objective, files, systems, risk, and protected boundaries.
2. Standing operator authority covers routine delivery mechanics inside that separate scope.

The standing grant cannot create or broaden product, production, county, PACS, SQL, secret,
deployment, destructive, legal, financial, or external-resource authority.

## Mechanical Classifier

The checked-in policy and verifier enforce this terminal rule:

```text
IF program_authorized
AND work_order_dependency_cleared
AND scope_within_authority
AND pr_open_non_draft
AND exact_head_assured
AND required_checks_complete
AND checks_green
AND unresolved_threads_zero
AND merge_state_permits
AND reservations_clear
AND no_true_authority_wall
THEN result = MERGE_AND_CONTINUE
```

Pending checks, review feedback, stale exact-head assurance, ordinary conflicts, and technical
uncertainty route to operator watch or remediation states. They do not become owner decisions.

`MERGE_AUTH_REQUIRED` may be emitted only when no applicable standing or bounded merge authority
exists.

## Portfolio Effect

The authority correction does not make a protected or unratified Work Order executable. The
evidence-backed `ALL_LANES_PARKED` portfolio result remains valid until a canonical program/Work
Order scope is admitted or a genuine protected wall receives separate authority. Once admitted, the
operator owns routine delivery and continuation without per-PR owner routing.
