## 🎯 **WHAT IS TERRAFUSION?**

Terrafusion is a **3-tier government marketplace platform** with AI-powered
property assessment, cost estimation, and complete county management systems.

### **🏗️ 3-Tier Architecture**

- **Tier 1**: Core Foundation (small counties)
- **Tier 2**: CostForge Professional (flagship product)
- **Tier 3**: Enterprise Suite (large counties)

### **🤖 AI-Powered Components**

- **50,000+ AI Agents** in hierarchical quantum-enhanced architecture
- **Supreme Commander Claude**: Global coordination and quantum optimization
- **Field Generals (1,220 Agents)**: Strategic operations management
- **Operational Forces (48,779 Agents)**: Execution and optimization
- **Claude-Flow Hive Minds (240+ Agents)**: Specialized government operations
- **Neural & Cognitive Systems**: 27+ neural models with government
  specialization
- **Claude-Flow Integration** with 87 MCP tools
