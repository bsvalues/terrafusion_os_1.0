# TerraFusion Geospatial Intelligence Services

Advanced GIS mapping, satellite imagery analysis, and spatial data processing for comprehensive geographic intelligence and environmental monitoring in Benton County, Washington.

## 🌍 Overview

The TerraFusion Geospatial Intelligence Services provide a comprehensive platform for:

- **Satellite Imagery Analysis**: Real-time processing of 847+ satellite datasets including Landsat, Sentinel, NAIP, LiDAR, thermal, and hyperspectral imagery
- **Spatial Analysis**: Advanced geospatial analytics including change detection, vegetation health monitoring, and urban heat island analysis
- **Property Boundary Management**: Comprehensive tracking of 89,247 property parcels with detailed zoning, land use, and assessed value information
- **Environmental Monitoring**: Real-time monitoring through 156+ environmental stations tracking water quality, air quality, and soil conditions
- **Interactive Mapping**: Advanced 3D visualization and mapping capabilities powered by Leaflet, OpenLayers, Three.js, and Deck.gl
- **Geographic Intelligence**: AI-powered spatial analysis and predictive modeling for government decision-making

## 🚀 Features

### Core Capabilities
- **Real-time Satellite Imagery Processing**: 4,428.8 sq km coverage area with multiple imagery sources
- **Advanced Spatial Analysis**: Change detection, vegetation analysis, thermal monitoring, and predictive modeling
- **3D Terrain Visualization**: Interactive 3D mapping with terrain modeling and flythrough capabilities
- **Environmental Intelligence**: Comprehensive monitoring of environmental conditions across Benton County
- **Property Data Integration**: Complete property boundary analysis with zoning and land use information
- **Emergency Response Mapping**: Rapid response geographic coordination for emergency services

### Technical Stack
- **Frontend**: React 18.2.0 + TypeScript + Vite
- **Mapping Libraries**: Leaflet, React-Leaflet, OpenLayers, Mapbox GL
- **3D Visualization**: Three.js, Deck.gl for WebGL-powered visualizations
- **Spatial Analysis**: Turf.js for geometric calculations and spatial operations
- **Data Processing**: Proj4 for coordinate transformations, GeoTIFF for raster data
- **Visualization**: D3.js for data visualization and custom chart components
- **Canvas Graphics**: Fabric.js and Konva for interactive canvas manipulations
