# TerraFusion Emergency Management & Disaster Response

Advanced incident command, emergency notifications, disaster response coordination, and crisis management system for Benton County, Washington.

## 🚨 Overview

TerraFusion Emergency Management provides comprehensive emergency response capabilities including:

- **Incident Command System**: Real-time incident tracking and command structure management
- **Emergency Alerts & Notifications**: Mass notification system with multiple delivery channels
- **Resource Management**: Emergency resource allocation and deployment tracking
- **Multi-Agency Coordination**: Unified command and interagency communication
- **Crisis Management**: Comprehensive disaster response and recovery operations
- **Public Safety Integration**: Coordination with fire, police, EMS, and other emergency services

## 🎯 Mission Critical Features

### Emergency Operations Center (EOC)
- **Real-time Incident Dashboard**: Live monitoring of active emergencies across Benton County
- **Command Structure**: Incident Commander assignment and response unit coordination
- **Resource Deployment**: Real-time tracking of emergency personnel and equipment
- **Geographic Intelligence**: Integration with mapping and GIS systems for spatial awareness

### Emergency Alert System
- **Wireless Emergency Alerts (WEA)**: Direct cellular notifications to affected populations
- **Siren Activation**: Coordinated warning siren deployment across 15 stations
- **Multi-Channel Communication**: Radio, social media, and door-to-door notifications
- **Evacuation Management**: Coordinated evacuation orders and route management

### Incident Management
- **Emergency Types**: Fire, flood, earthquake, severe weather, hazmat, medical, security, infrastructure, cyber, and pandemic response
- **Alert Levels**: Minimal, Minor, Moderate, Major, and Extreme severity classifications
- **Status Tracking**: Active, monitoring, contained, resolved, and closed incident states
- **Impact Assessment**: Population impact and damage estimation

6. **Situation Assessment**: Ongoing impact and resource evaluation
