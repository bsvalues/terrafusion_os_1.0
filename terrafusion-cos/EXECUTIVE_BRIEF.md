## 🎯 ONE-PAGE SUMMARY

### **What is TerraFusion cOS?**

TerraFusion cOS is a **vendor substrate platform** - like AWS for government technology. It's NOT a SaaS product sold to counties. Instead, major vendors (Harris, Tyler, Esri, Woolpert) license cOS to build their own government solutions on top of it.

### **The Breakthrough Insight**

Instead of competing with vendors (Harris) by selling to counties, TerraFusion **empowers vendors** by giving them the infrastructure layer they desperately need. Harris wins. We win. Counties win.
