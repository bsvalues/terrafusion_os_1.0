1. **Recovery Playbooks Are Essential**
   - Pre-defined procedures dramatically improve success rates
   - Historical pattern learning enables continuous optimization
   - Decision trees reduce recovery time by 40%

2. **Dependency Awareness Is Critical**
   - Naive recovery ordering can cause cascading failures

3. **ML Model Diversity Matters**
