## 🤝 INTERVIEW & EVALUATION PROCESS

### **Multi-Stage Interview Process**

#### **Stage 1: Initial Screening (30 minutes)**
- Technical background and experience
- Interest in government technology sector
- Motivation for leaving current role
- Salary and equity expectations
- Cultural fit assessment

#### **Stage 2: Technical Interview (90 minutes)**
- System design challenges relevant to government software
- Code review and architecture discussion
- Problem-solving approach evaluation
- Technology stack knowledge assessment

#### **Stage 3: Team Fit Interview (60 minutes)**
- Meet with potential team members
- Collaborative problem-solving exercise
- Communication and leadership skills
- Values alignment discussion

#### **Stage 4: Leadership Interview (45 minutes)**
- Meet with founder/leadership team
- Vision alignment and growth potential
- Equity and compensation discussion
- Role definition and expectations

### **Technical Assessment Areas**
```
Core Competencies:
├── Web application development (React, Vue, Angular)
├── Backend systems (Node.js, Python, Java, C#)
├── Database design and optimization (PostgreSQL, SQL Server)
├── API design and integration
├── Cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP)
├── DevOps and CI/CD pipelines
└── Security and compliance considerations

Government Software Specific:
├── GIS and mapping technologies
├── Large dataset processing and analytics
├── Workflow automation and business rules
├── Document management and generation
├── Integration with legacy systems
├── Compliance and audit trail requirements
└── Multi-tenant architecture patterns
```
