MATHEMATICAL PRIVACY EXPLAINED

Differential Privacy Formula:
  A mechanism M is (ε, δ)-DP if for any adjacent datasets D, D':

  Pr[M(D) ∈ S] ≤ e^ε × Pr[M(D') ∈ S] + δ

  Interpretation:
  - ε controls maximum probability change (0.01-1.0 typical)
  - δ controls failure probability (1e-5 to 1e-8 typical)
  - Guarantees hold against all possible attacks
  - Smaller ε = stronger privacy

Practical Example:
  Analyst queries: "How many citizens support policy X?"

  If there are 1,000,000 responses total:

  Without Privacy: Result = 450,000 (exact count)
  With DP:         Result = 450,002.7 (exact + noise)

  Guarantee: Cannot identify if specific person's response included
