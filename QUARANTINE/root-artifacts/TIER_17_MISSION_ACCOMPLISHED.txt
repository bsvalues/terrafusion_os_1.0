All Tier 17 engines provide FORMAL PRIVACY GUARANTEES:

Differential Privacy: (ε, δ)-Differential Privacy
  "Output is (ε, δ)-DP if removing any one person's data changes
   probability of output by at most e^ε factor (with δ exceptions)"

  Configuration:
  • Public Data:       ε = 5.0,  δ = 1e-5
  • Internal Data:     ε = 1.0,  δ = 1e-6
  • Confidential Data: ε = 0.1,  δ = 1e-7
  • Restricted Data:   ε = 0.01, δ = 1e-8

Federated Learning: (ε, δ)-DP Training
  (10.0, 1e-5)-DP across entire training
  Individual data never centralized
  Only aggregated differentially-private updates shared
