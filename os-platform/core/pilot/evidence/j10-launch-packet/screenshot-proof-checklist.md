# June 10 Screenshot Proof Checklist

## Purpose

Capture proof for the exact June 10 demo path. Do not use screenshots to imply broader production readiness.

## Required Screenshots

- Login or entry screen.
- Benton County selected as `Runtime Pilot`.
- Top bar showing county, role, department/context, and runtime status.
- Property Workbench entry.
- Benton parcel/property summary from TerraFusion DB/API-backed data.
- CAMA/improvement/property characteristics where sourced and defensible.
- Sales/comparable surface only if real, sourced, and included in the demo path.
- Evidence/provenance note showing Benton PACS-derived source posture.
- Runtime identity or health proof if available in the rehearsal environment.
- Snapshot/evidence/manifest posture.
- Non-Benton county selected.
- Non-Benton onboarding/provenance workspace.
- Non-Benton runtime operation blocked or labeled `Not Runtime Enabled`.
- Final claim sheet.

## Screenshot Rules

- Capture only the locked demo path.
- Do not capture placeholder modules as production-ready.
- Do not capture AI valuation outputs unless they are sourced and explicitly approved.
- Do not capture PACS Bridge as a live Hostinger dependency.
- Do not capture non-Benton counties as runtime-enabled.
- Label any preview/unavailable/onboarding surfaces visibly.

## Evidence Directory

Recommended storage:

```text
os-platform/core/pilot/evidence/j10-launch-packet/screenshots/
```

Screenshot files are optional in this packet until captured during live rehearsal. This checklist is the acceptance control for what must be captured before the June 10 presentation.

## Readiness Mark

The screenshot set may be marked `READY_FOR_DEMO` only when every captured image matches the final claim sheet and no image implies a forbidden claim.
