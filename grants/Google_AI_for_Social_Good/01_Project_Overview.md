- **Digital Divide:** Small counties can't afford modern technology, creating
  service inequality
- **Innovation Stagnation:** Vendor lock-in prevents customization and

approaches perpetuate inequality:

  requirements
- **Limited accessibility** means AI benefits concentrate in wealthy, urban
  areas
- **No ecosystem approach** prevents counties from collaborating and sharing
  innovations

**Terrafusion's AI for Social Good Approach:**
