## ✅ How to wire it (1–2 hours, end-to-end)

1. **CDN (CloudFront + your media origin)**

   * Set `var.domain_name` to your media origin (e.g., Kong route or Nginx host).
   * `terraform init && terraform apply` in `infra/cloudfront/`.
   * Result: `/media/*` served with immutable cache, compression, and CSP headers.

2. **Preloads**

   * Import `<RoutePreloads route={location.pathname}/>` in your app shell.
   * Keep the map in `assetsForRoute()` aligned with `scenes.ts`.
   * Target: first motion frame ≤1200 ms desktop, ≤1800 ms mobile.

3. **Workerized WebGL (T4 path)**

   * In your WebGL layer, prefer `WebGLLayerWorker` when `transferControlToOffscreen` exists; otherwise keep current `WebGLLayer`.
   * Offload your heavy scenes by creating `*.offscreen.js` modules exporting `initOffscreen(canvas, params)`.
   * Acceptance: **main-thread long tasks <50 ms p95** while T4 is active.

4. **Context loss & demotion**

   * Bind `bindContextLoss(canvas, onLost)` and demote to T2 on loss; auto-restore when context returns.
   * Emit `trackExperience('webgl_context_lost', {...})` for SRE alerting.

5. **CSP + Security**

   * Add `CspHeadersMiddleware` to ASP.NET Core pipeline (`app.UseMiddleware<CspHeadersMiddleware>();`).
   * Merge `kong.csp.yml` into your Kong declarative config.
   * Keep `style-src 'unsafe-inline'` only if your current CSS pipeline needs it; we can tighten with hashes later.

6. **Telemetry & Exec tiles**

   * Run `ops/db/telemetry.sql` on your DB (Postgres assumed).
   * Register `TelemetryRepository` in DI, add `TelemetrySummaryController`.
   * Mount `<ExecExperienceTiles/>` in your Exec dashboard.
   * Now you can watch tier mix shift by route / minute window.

7. **SRE controls**

   * Implement feature flags (`transcendence.globalEnabled`, `forceTier`, `maxTier`) in your config store.
   * Use the included runbook to brownout to T1 during incidents, and verify via `/api/telemetry/summary`.
