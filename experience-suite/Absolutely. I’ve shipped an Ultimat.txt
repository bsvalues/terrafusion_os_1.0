## Wire-up (fast & deterministic)

1. **DB & Audit**

   * Run: `psql $DATABASE_URL -f ops/db/flag_audit.sql`.
   * DI: register `FlagAuditRepository`.
   * In your flag `SetAsync` path, call `InsertAsync(key, json, actor, reason)` and `GrafanaAnnotator.WriteAsync("Flag change", { key, actor })`.

2. **Admin**

   * Route `/admin/flag-history` → `FlagHistory.tsx`. Pair with the existing Transcendence Control page.

3. **FMF (first motion frame)**

   * In your Video/WebGL layer, call `markMotionStart()` when the first frame is actually rendered or video `playing`.
   * Run Lighthouse with `tools/lh-terrafusion.config.json`; fail CI if FMF > 1200ms.

4. **Visual regression**

   * Merge the Playwright spec; add baseline snapshots on a golden build; keep them under review with CODEOWNERS.

5. **ESLint brand rule**

   * Extend your ESLint config with `tools/eslintrc.terrafusion.json` or add `"plugin:terrafusion/recommended"`.
   * CI: `eslint .` must be green.

6. **Prometheus + Grafana**

   * Apply `ServiceMonitor` if using Prometheus Operator.
   * Use prior dashboards; annotations will now show flag flips.

7. **CSP reporting (Report-To/NEL)**

   * Add `ReportToHeaders` middleware with CSP controllers from earlier packs to aggregate violations cleanly.

8. **CloudFront invalidation**

   * `./tools/cf-invalidate.sh <DIST_ID> '/media/*'` after deploying new media.
