# Benton County, Washington - Terrafusion Profile

## County Overview
- **Location**: South-central Washington State
- **County Seat**: Prosser
- **Established**: March 8, 1905
- **FIPS Code**: 53005
- **Area**: 1,703 square miles (1,700 land, 3 water)
- **Population**: 206,873 (2020 Census)

## Geographic Features
- **Elevation Range**: 285 feet (Columbia River) to 3,600 feet (Rattlesnake Hills)
- **Major Rivers**: Columbia River, Yakima River, Snake River
- **Climate**: Semi-arid desert with hot, dry summers and mild winters
- **Region**: Columbia River Valley, Horse Heaven Hills

## Major Cities and Communities
1. **Kennewick** - Population 83,920 (largest city)
   - Incorporated: 1904
   - Economic hub of Tri-Cities area
   - Major industries: Nuclear, agriculture, food processing

2. **Richland** - Population 60,560
   - Incorporated: 1958
   - Home to Pacific Northwest National Laboratory
   - Hanford Nuclear Site operations

3. **West Richland** - Population 15,875
   - Incorporated: 1955
   - Residential community
   - Growing tech sector

4. **Prosser** - Population 6,062 (County Seat)
   - Incorporated: 1899
   - Wine country center
   - Administrative hub

5. **Benton City** - Population 3,548
   - Incorporated: 1909
   - Agricultural community
   - Historic railroad town

## Economic Profile

### Major Industries
- **Nuclear Energy**: Hanford Nuclear Reservation (586 sq miles)
- **Agriculture**: Wine grapes, wheat, potatoes, onions, corn
- **Food Processing**: Agricultural product processing
- **Technology**: Research and development
- **Manufacturing**: Aerospace, electronics
- **Tourism**: Wine country, recreational boating

### Agricultural Production
- **Wine Industry**: 200+ wineries, premium wine grape production
- **Wheat**: Soft white wheat for export markets
- **Potatoes**: Processing and fresh market varieties
- **Specialty Crops**: Onions, corn, alfalfa, cherries
