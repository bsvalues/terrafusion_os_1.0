## 🔄 **SERVICE COMMUNICATION PATTERNS**

### **Synchronous Communication**
- **HTTP/HTTPS**: Direct service-to-service calls
- **gRPC**: High-performance internal communication
- **GraphQL**: Flexible data querying across services

### **Asynchronous Communication**
- **Event-Driven**: Domain events for loose coupling
- **Message Queues**: Reliable async processing
- **Event Streaming**: Real-time data flows
- **Pub/Sub**: Broadcast notifications

### **Data Consistency Patterns**
- **Saga Pattern**: Distributed transactions
- **Event Sourcing**: Immutable event log
- **CQRS**: Command/Query segregation
- **Eventual Consistency**: Acceptable for most government operations
