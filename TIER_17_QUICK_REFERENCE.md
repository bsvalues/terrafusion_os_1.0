- **Federated Averaging (FedAvg)**: Collaborative ML training algorithm
- **Gradient Clipping**: Clip to norm ≤ 1.0 for privacy protection
- **DP-SGD Integration**: Differential privacy during aggregation
- **Multi-Party Support**: Up to 5-party federations
- **Privacy Guarantee**: (10.0 ε, 1e-5 δ)-DP across all training

- **Use Case**: Compute on encrypted health/financial data without decryption

- **Information Leakage**: DP-based quantification
- **Inference Attack Vulnerability**: Model accuracy gap analysis

- **Auto-Recommendations**: Risk mitigation guidance
