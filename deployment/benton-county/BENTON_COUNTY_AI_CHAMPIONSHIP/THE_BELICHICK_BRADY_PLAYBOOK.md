## 🚨 EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS

### Red Alert: System Down

**Trigger**: Any core service unavailable >30 seconds

**Response Protocol**:

1. **Immediate**: Auto-failover to backup systems
2. **Notification**: Alert all stakeholders within 60 seconds
3. **Assessment**: Full system diagnostic within 5 minutes
4. **Resolution**: Primary system restore within 15 minutes
5. **Analysis**: Post-incident review within 24 hours

### Orange Alert: Performance Degradation

**Trigger**: Response times >500ms for >2 minutes

**Response Protocol**:

1. **Analysis**: Identify bottleneck within 2 minutes
2. **Mitigation**: Apply performance optimization
3. **Monitoring**: Enhanced surveillance for 1 hour
4. **Optimization**: Permanent fix within 24 hours

### Yellow Alert: Capacity Warning

**Trigger**: System utilization >80% for >5 minutes

**Response Protocol**:

1. **Scaling**: Auto-scale resources immediately
2. **Load Balancing**: Redistribute workload
3. **Monitoring**: Track utilization trends
4. **Planning**: Capacity expansion if needed
