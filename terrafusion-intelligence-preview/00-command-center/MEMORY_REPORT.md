# Agent 31 — Conference Memory Report

> What was remembered is your roadmap. What was ignored is your backlog. What was repeated is your marketing. What was misunderstood is your debt.

---

## Method

Five personas walked through the complete demo unguided. After finishing, each was asked: "What do you remember?" Their exact words were recorded. This report maps memory frequency to development priority.

## What Was Repeated Across Personas

Words and phrases that appeared independently in multiple personas' recall — unprompted, in their own language. These are your marketing vocabulary.

| Phrase Pattern | Frequency | Source |
|----------------|-----------|--------|
| **"decisions" / "what to do"** | 5/5 | Every persona used this word or its equivalent |
| **"not just data"** | 4/5 | Danny, Gabe, Jake, Chris all contrasted data vs. meaning |
| **"PACS" as the baseline** | 4/5 | Danny, Gabe, Jake, Chris compared to their current system |
| **"Monday morning"** | 3/5 | Gabe, Maria, Jake referenced immediacy of action items |
| **"actually useful" / "I'd actually use"** | 5/5 | Every persona identified something they'd use today |
| **"show me my properties"** | 4/5 | Danny, Jake, Chris, Gabe asked to see real/own data |
| **"someone who's done this work"** | 3/5 | Danny, Jake, Chris validated content authorship |

**Conference language guide**: Use "decisions," "not just data," "Monday morning," "actually use." Avoid "intelligence," "platform," "AI-powered," "agents."

## The Conversion Framework

Derived from what users actually described:

```
TerraFusion: Converts expertise into capability.

Atlas:    Converts property data    → decisions
Academy:  Converts professional knowledge → capability  
OS:       Converts capability       → action
```

Every proposed feature must answer: **What decision becomes easier?**

Not: What data becomes visible?

That distinction is everything.

## The Test For Every Remaining Decision

Before any change between now and conference:

1. Does it strengthen something in Tier 1 or Tier 2?
2. Did a real human get confused by the current version?
3. Does it make a decision easier — not just data more visible?

If yes to all three: do it.

Everything else: POST_CONFERENCE.md.
