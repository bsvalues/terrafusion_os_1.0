# Demo Script

Two timings. Both follow the locked story: **Atlas → Academy → OS.**
Goal is transmission, not presentation — the product demonstrates, you narrate less.

---

## 3-Minute Script (the proof)

**0:00 — Frame (15s)**
"TerraFusion helps people understand what matters, why it matters, and what to do next.
Three quick looks: a property, a problem, and an action."

**0:15 — Atlas: understand a property (60s)**
- Open the Atlas Property Dossier on one Benton County parcel.
- Point only at: Facts → Context → Signals → Interpretation → **Now What?**
- Line: "This isn't data. It's a read on the property."

**1:15 — Academy: understand a problem (60s)**
- Open one Codex entry (e.g. Senior Exemption Audit).
- Point at: Problem → Why it matters → How experts think → **Now What?**
- Line: "This is how an experienced professional approaches it — transferable."

**2:15 — OS: act (30s)**
- Show the handoff: from understanding to an action the office can take.
- Line: "Understand the property, understand the problem, know the next step."

**2:45 — Close (15s)**
"That's TerraFusion: capability, transferred."

---

## 10-Minute Script (the walkthrough)

1. **Frame (1m)** — the one sentence + the three questions.
2. **Atlas Dossier (3m)** — walk all five sections on one parcel; linger on Signals + Now What.
3. **County Pulse (2m)** — Growth · Permits · Development · Risk Watch · Top 5 to Watch · Now What.
4. **Academy Codex (2m)** — one entry end to end; emphasize "How experts think."
5. **Ask Academy (1m)** — one real question, one credible answer.
6. **OS Handoff + Close (1m)** — understanding → action; restate the mission sentence.

---

## Rehearsal rule
Every rehearsal should reduce how much YOU explain and increase how much the product shows.
Every answer gets shorter. Every transition gets cleaner.
