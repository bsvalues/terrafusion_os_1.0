# Demo Scripts

---

## 3-Minute Script

### Opening (30 seconds)
> "Property assessment professionals deal with three questions every day:
> What should I know about this property?
> How would an expert approach this problem?
> What should my office do next?
> TerraFusion answers all three."

### Atlas — Understand a Property (60 seconds)
- Open Atlas search
- Enter demo parcel
- Show Property Dossier: Facts, Context, Signals, Interpretation, Now What
- "This isn't just data. It's intelligence."

### Academy — Understand a Problem (60 seconds)
- Open Academy Codex
- Show Senior Exemption Audit entry
- Walk through: Problem, Why It Matters, How Experts Think, Now What
- "This is how a 20-year veteran would teach you."

### TerraFusion OS — Act on Intelligence (30 seconds)
- Show OS dashboard
- Demonstrate handoff from Atlas insight to action
- "Atlas tells you what matters. Academy tells you how to think about it. TerraFusion OS tells you what to do next."

### Close (15 seconds)
> "Intelligence. Not just data."

## 10-Minute Script

### Opening (1 minute)
- The three questions framing
- Brief context on county assessment challenges

### Atlas Deep Dive (3 minutes)
- Property search flow
- Full dossier walkthrough with each section
- County Pulse: growth signals, permit activity, risk watch
- "Now What?" actionable intelligence

### Academy Deep Dive (3 minutes)
- Codex browse/search
- Senior Exemption Audit — full walkthrough
- BOE Preparation — brief overview
- Ask Academy: type a question, get expert-level guidance
- "This replaces years of mentorship time."

### TerraFusion OS Integration (2 minutes)
- Dashboard overview
- Intelligence-to-action pipeline
- Multi-county capability mention (do not demo)

### Close (1 minute)
- Recap three questions
- "Available for Washington State counties"
- Contact / next steps

---

## Presenter Notes

- Never say "AI" without showing what it does
- Never demo a loading spinner — pre-warm everything
- If anything breaks, switch to fallback PDFs immediately
- Practice transitions between Atlas → Academy → OS
