## Agent 27 — Dumb Smart Person Checklist

Before the conference, a non-assessor should walk through the demo cold and answer:

### At /demo
- [ ] Do I understand what these three cards mean without explanation?
- [ ] Do I know which one to click first?
- [ ] Is there any jargon on this page?

### At /atlas/dossier/demo
- [ ] Can I read the Intelligence Summary and understand it?
- [ ] Do I know what "assessed value" means? (If not, is context provided?)
- [ ] Do the signal titles make sense to me without domain knowledge?
- [ ] Does "Now What?" feel actionable even if I'm not an assessor?
- [ ] Can I find the back button / navigation?

### At /atlas/county-pulse/demo
- [ ] Does the County Intelligence Summary make sense?
- [ ] Do I understand the growth signals without explanation?
- [ ] Is "Risk Watch" clear to a non-expert?

### At /academy

- [ ] Can I tell which entry is for beginners vs. advanced?
- [ ] Is the "Ask Academy" card visible and inviting?

- [ ] Can I follow the content without assessment experience?
- [ ] Is the section structure (Problem → Why → How → Workflow → Tools → Mistakes → Now What) intuitive?
- [ ] Do I feel smarter after reading one entry?

- [ ] Do I know what to type?
- [ ] Is the response useful even if I'm not an assessor?

- [ ] What's the one thing I remember?
- [ ] Would I ask "Can I use this?"
