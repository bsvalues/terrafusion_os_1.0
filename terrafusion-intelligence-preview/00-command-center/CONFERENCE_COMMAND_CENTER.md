## The Two-Week Campaign

### Phase 1: Internal Rehearsal (Days 1-4)

Run the 3-minute and 10-minute presentations. Repeatedly.

Track: Where do I ramble? Where do I drift into architecture? Where do I get excited about the wrong things?

Goal: Shorter. Clearer. Simpler. Every day.

### Phase 2: Live Fire Validation (Days 5-9)

Real humans. Not agents.

Ask only: What do you remember? What confused you? What would you ask next?

**The Danny/Gabe Transmission Exercise**: Ask each to explain the product to someone who hasn't seen it. Don't help. Don't correct. Just listen.

### Phase 3: Conference Lock (Days 10-14)

No changes. No features. No pivots.

Only: confidence, timing, transitions, objection handling.

## How the Doctrine Was Built

```
User Experience → Memory → Language → Doctrine
```

Not:

```
Doctrine → Language → Product → Hope
```

That's why it feels real. Five personas used the product independently. We recorded what they remembered. We studied what language they used. The doctrine is what emerged.

## The Final Test

Not downloads. Not signups. Not applause.

**Did people leave understanding what TerraFusion does, without anyone having to explain it to them?**

And then:

**Did they explain it to someone else in the right words?**

If yes: the product is teaching people how to talk about itself.

That is the hardest thing to achieve. And the most valuable.
