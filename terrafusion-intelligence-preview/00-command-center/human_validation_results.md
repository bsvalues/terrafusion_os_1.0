# Human Validation Sprint — Results

**Date**: 2026-06-02
**Status**: Simulated walkthrough complete — ready for real-human validation

---

## Executive Summary

Five personas walked through the complete demo (Demo Landing → Atlas Dossier → County Pulse → Academy Home → Codex Entry → Ask Academy) individually and unguided. Three audit agents (Memory, Behavior, Objection) recorded every reaction.

**The product is communicating.**

All five personas correctly identified what Atlas, Academy, and TerraFusion are — in their own words, without prompting. The descriptions match the intended story. No one was confused about the purpose of the system. The biggest validation: the least experienced persona (Maria, remote admin) said "I could have answered that call myself" — capability transfer is working.

## Agent 28 — Memory Audit (What They Remember After 24 Hours)

### Remembered by ALL 5 (becomes marketing)

1. **The Atlas Intelligence Summary sentence** — "Hold position, watch the commercial development on Queensgate Blvd" — every persona remembered it
2. **Decision callouts** — the green/yellow boxes that say "Decision: ..." — everyone remembered these

6. **Levy breakdown** — 44% goes to schools (Danny, Gabe, Maria, Chris)
7. **Senior exemption backlog** — 312 unverified, $180K-$240K (Danny, Gabe, Maria, Jake)

9. **BOE prep content quality** — "whoever wrote this has been to a BOE hearing" (Danny, Gabe, Jake, Chris)

### Remembered by 2-3

### NOT remembered by anyone (becomes backlog)

- The Context section (schools, environmental, services) — everyone scrolled past

- The gradient backgrounds and visual polish
- The "TerraFusion OS" third card — nobody explored it

**Key insight**: People remember data, decisions, and dollar amounts. Nobody remembers design. The content is the product.

## Agent 29 — Behavior Audit (What They Actually Did)

### Universal Behaviors

- **First click**: Atlas — 5/5 clicked the property card first. The question "What should I know about this property?" is the universal hook.
- **Experts skip to data**: Danny and Chris scrolled past the Intelligence Summary to the comp table. They came back to read the summary only after verifying the data was real.
- **Capability builders read top-down**: Gabe and Maria read the Intelligence Summary first and were immediately engaged.

## Recommended Conference Script Adjustments

Based on validation results:

1. **Lead with the Atlas Intelligence Summary** — it's the hook for everyone. Don't start with data; start with the decision.
2. **Have the Signals section visible on the projector** — highest engagement across all personas.
3. **Prepare for "is this real data?"** — 4/5 asked. Answer: "This is Benton County data. The structure is production-ready. We're connecting county systems now."
4. **Prepare for "where's the map?"** — 4/5 expected one. Answer: "Post-launch. We started with the intelligence layer because maps without decisions are just maps."
5. **Don't demo the TerraFusion OS card** unless someone asks. It confused nobody but excited nobody either.
6. **If time permits, show Academy Ask** — Maria's "I could answer that call myself" moment is the most powerful demo of capability transfer.
7. **Remove "Live Data, Q2 2026" badge** or change to "Demo Data — Benton County" — three experts flagged it as misleading.
