# Conference Evidence Package

> People did not remember the software. They remembered the decisions.

---

## Validation

Five personas walked through the demo individually and unguided. All five independently described the product the way we intended it to be described.

### The Five Quotes

**#1** — "PACS gives me data. This gives me decisions."
*— Chris, Field Appraiser (8 years)*

Positions TerraFusion without attacking PACS. Explains the difference immediately.

**#2** — "I don't feel like an expert. But I feel like I could become one."
*— Gabe, Learning Appraiser (1.5 years)*

Academy's mission statement. Not literally. Philosophically.

**#3** — "I could have answered that call myself."
*— Maria, Remote Admin (2 years)*

Capability transfer in one sentence.

**#4** — "Someone finally built the tool I've been describing in my head for three years."
*— Jake, Technical Analyst (4 years)*

The problem is real. Someone recognized it.

**#5** — "This is the first time I've seen signals, risk prioritization, and BOE defense logic built into a property view."
*— Danny, Senior Assessor (15 years)*

Expert validation. Important internally. Too technical for marketing.

### The Hidden Pattern

None of the five mentioned: AI, agents, architecture, RAG, MCP, or marketplace.

Users responded to outcomes. Not technology.

---

## What People Remembered

- Atlas Intelligence Summary ("Hold position, watch the commercial development")
- Decision callouts (the green/yellow boxes that say "Decision: ...")
- Badger Mountain COD 18.2
- Comparable sales ratios (four comps, all within 2%)
- "Now What?" action items
- Levy breakdown (44% goes to schools)
- Senior exemption backlog ($180K-$240K)
- HB 2147 legislative impact ($2.1M)

**Pattern**: Decisions and dollar amounts.

---

## What People Did NOT Care About

- Design, animations, gradients, transitions
- Architecture or technical implementation
- Color coding and category filters
- The "TerraFusion OS" third card (0/5 clicked it)
- Property Snapshot details (familiar PACS data)
- Context section (schools, environmental, services)

**Pattern**: Nobody remembers how it looks. Everybody remembers what it says.

---

## Top Requested Features (All Post-Conference)

1. **Map** — 4/5 expected one
2. **Search** — 4/5 wanted to type a parcel number
3. **Export/Print** — 5/5 would use this
4. **Photos** — 3/5 expected property images
5. **Glossary/Tooltips** — 2/5 needed inline term definitions

These are post-conference. They validate demand, not urgency.

## Conference Talking Points

**When someone asks "Is this real data?"**

"This is demo data based on a real Benton County property. The structure is production-ready. We're connecting county systems now."

Don't hide it. Lead with it. That increases credibility.

**When someone asks "Can this run on my county?"**

Today: Demo data.
Next: County-specific data.
Eventually: Live county intelligence.

Now they can see the path.

**When someone asks "What about maps / photos / export?"**

"Those are on the roadmap. We started with the intelligence layer because maps without decisions are just maps."

## Where To Invest Conference Energy

**Double down on**: Signals. Decisions. Now What. Dollar amounts.

**Stop spending energy on**: Design polish. Animations. Transitions. Architecture slides.

The content is the product.

## Internal Compass

This document is the compass for every conference decision from now until demo day.

When someone proposes a change, ask:

1. Does it add a decision, a dollar amount, or a "Now What?"
2. Did a real human get confused by the current version?
3. Can it be explained in one sentence?

If yes to all three: do it.
If no to any: POST_CONFERENCE.md.
