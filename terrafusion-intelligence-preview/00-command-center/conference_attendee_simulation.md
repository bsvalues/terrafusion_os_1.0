## Cross-Persona Analysis

### Universal Strengths
1. **Atlas Intelligence Summary** — every persona found it immediately valuable
2. **"Now What?" framing** — converts understanding into action for every audience
3. **Expert-level content** — assessment professionals recognized authentic domain knowledge
4. **Zero-friction demo** — no login, no loading, no errors. Just works.

### Universal Gaps
1. **"How does it connect to what I already use?"** — 3 of 5 personas asked about integration
2. **"What does implementation look like?"** — 2 of 5 personas need a deployment story
3. **"Can I customize it for my county?"** — 2 of 5 personas want county-specific content
4. **"What's the cost?"** — the commissioner and the chief deputy will ask this

### Who Will Ask For Access?
- **Certain**: Assessor, Chief Deputy, Staff Appraiser (3/5)
- **Likely**: Commissioner (as a budget decision for the office)
- **Interested**: Investor (wants public-facing version)

### The One-Line Takeaway Each Persona Leaves With

| Persona | Takeaway |
|---------|----------|
| Assessor | "This is the system I've been describing to vendors for a decade." |
| Chief Deputy | "This could cut my BOE prep time in half." |
| Staff Appraiser | "Everything I need for a field inspection, in one screen." |
| Investor | "I trust this county's assessments more after seeing this." |

## Part 2: Capability Builder Personas

The expert personas above judge accuracy, depth, and credibility. But the real test of "capability transfer" is whether people who DON'T already have the capability walk away with more of it. These personas are a harsher test of clarity — and may be more important for Academy.

---

## Persona 6: Remote Admin Assistant (Supports 3 appraisers, 2 years in the office)

### Atlas Dossier Experience
**What impressed me**: "I finally understand what my appraisers are looking at. When they talk about comps and ratios, I nod along. But this — I can actually see how the pieces connect. The assessed value, the comparable sales, the levy breakdown. It makes sense as a picture."

**What confused me**: "What's a COD? The signals mention IAAO standards — I don't know what those are. The signal titles made sense but some of the detail paragraphs lost me."

**What I want next**: "Can I pull this up when a taxpayer calls? Half my job is fielding phone calls from property owners who want to know why their tax went up. If I could show them this — or even just read from it — I'd sound competent instead of transferring to an appraiser."

**Would I ask for access?**: YES. "This would make me better at my job tomorrow."

**Do I feel more confident?**: "Yes. I've worked here two years and this is the first time the assessment process actually clicked for me."

### Academy Experience
**What impressed me**: "The Senior Exemption Audit entry. I process exemption applications every week but I never understood the strategy behind what we're doing. Now I get it — we're not just checking boxes, we're protecting levy equity."

**What confused me**: "Some entries assume I know things I don't. 'REETA' in the Sales Validation entry — I had to guess what that was. But the structure helped: Problem → Why It Matters walked me through the reasoning."

**What I want next**: "A 'Fundamentals' track. Entries written for people like me who support the work but weren't trained as appraisers."

**Would I ask for access?**: YES — emphatically. "I'd read one entry every morning with my coffee."

**Do I feel more confident?**: "Much more. I understand WHY we do things, not just WHAT we do."

### TerraFusion OS Experience
**What impressed me**: "The home screen is clear. I know where things are."

**What confused me**: "'Dais'? What does that word mean? And 'Consciousness' in the footer — is that AI? That's intimidating."

**Would I ask for access?**: YES, for Atlas and Academy. "The OS is for my boss. Atlas and Academy are for me."

## Persona 7: New Hire (First week in an assessor's office, no assessment background)

**Would I ask for access?**: YES. "This is better than the training manual they gave me, which I couldn't follow."

**Do I feel more confident?**: "Yes. Not confident enough to do the work yet — but confident enough to ask the right questions. Before this, I didn't even know what questions to ask."
