## RISK ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

### Automated Risk Scoring
- **Data Sensitivity Score** (0-20): Classification-based
- **Access Breadth Score** (0-20): Number of users/systems
- **Encryption Status** (0-20): Encryption strength and compliance
- **Update Status** (0-20): Patch currency and security posture
- **Compliance History** (0-20): Past violations and remediation
- **Overall Risk**: Average of all factors (0-100)

### Risk Levels
- **Critical** (80-100): Immediate remediation required
- **High** (60-79): Urgent remediation within 24 hours
- **Medium** (40-59): Planned remediation within 7 days
- **Low** (20-39): Routine remediation within 30 days
- **Minimal** (<20): No immediate action needed

### Vulnerability Identification
- Weak encryption detected
- Patches not current
- Excessive access breadth
- Compliance violations
- Configuration drift
- Authentication weaknesses
