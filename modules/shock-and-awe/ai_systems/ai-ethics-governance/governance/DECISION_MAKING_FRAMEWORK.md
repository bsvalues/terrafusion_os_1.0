# AI Decision-Making Framework
## TerraFusion Government AI Systems

### Overview
This framework establishes standardized processes for ethical decision-making in AI system development, deployment, and operation within government property assessment services.

### Decision Categories

#### Category 1: Routine Operational Decisions
**Examples**: Minor algorithm updates, standard data processing changes
**Authority**: Technical Team Lead
**Process**: Internal review and documentation
**Timeline**: 1-2 business days

#### Category 2: Significant System Changes
**Examples**: New feature additions, assessment methodology changes
**Authority**: AI Ethics Committee (Simple Majority)
**Process**: Technical review → Ethics evaluation → Committee vote
**Timeline**: 2-4 weeks

#### Category 3: High-Impact Decisions
**Examples**: New AI model deployment, major policy changes
**Authority**: AI Ethics Committee (Two-thirds Majority)
**Process**: Full ethical review → Public comment → Committee deliberation → Vote
**Timeline**: 6-8 weeks

#### Category 4: Emergency Decisions
**Examples**: System shutdown, immediate bias correction
**Authority**: Committee Chair or Emergency Response Team
**Process**: Immediate action → Post-action review within 48 hours
**Timeline**: Immediate

### Ethical Decision-Making Process

#### Step 1: Problem Identification
- Clear definition of the issue or opportunity
- Stakeholder impact assessment
- Legal and regulatory considerations
- Technical feasibility evaluation

#### Step 2: Ethical Analysis
**Stakeholder Impact Assessment**
- Citizens affected by the decision
- Government operations impact
- Community and economic effects
- Long-term societal implications

**Rights and Values Evaluation**
- Human rights considerations
- Privacy and data protection
- Fairness and non-discrimination
- Transparency and accountability

**Risk Assessment**
- Potential for bias or discrimination
- Privacy and security risks
- Operational and reputational risks
- Unintended consequences analysis

#### Step 3: Alternative Development
- Generate multiple solution options
- Evaluate ethical implications of each
- Consider "do nothing" option
- Assess resource requirements

#### Step 4: Consultation Process
**Internal Consultation**
- Technical team review
- Legal counsel assessment
- Data protection officer input
- Senior management review

**External Consultation** (when applicable)
- Community representative input
- Subject matter expert advice
- Peer jurisdiction consultation
- Academic research review

#### Step 5: Decision Documentation
**Required Documentation**
- Problem statement and context
- Ethical analysis summary
- Stakeholder consultation results
- Risk assessment and mitigation plans
- Decision rationale and alternatives considered

### Risk Assessment Matrix

#### Risk Levels
**Low Risk**: Minimal citizen impact, routine operations
**Medium Risk**: Moderate citizen impact, reversible consequences
**High Risk**: Significant citizen impact, potential for discrimination
**Critical Risk**: Major citizen impact, irreversible consequences

#### Risk Factors
1. **Scale of Impact**: Number of citizens affected
2. **Severity of Consequences**: Magnitude of potential harm
3. **Reversibility**: Ability to undo or correct decisions
4. **Transparency**: Ability to explain and justify decisions
5. **Precedent Setting**: Implications for future decisions

### Approval Workflows

#### Standard Approval Process
```
Request Submission → Initial Review → Technical Assessment → Ethics Review → 
Stakeholder Consultation → Committee Deliberation → Decision → Implementation → Monitoring
```

#### Expedited Process (Medium Risk or Below)
```
Request Submission → Combined Technical/Ethics Review → 
Committee Chair Approval → Implementation → Post-Implementation Review
```

#### Emergency Process
```
Immediate Assessment → Emergency Authority Decision → 
Immediate Implementation → Post-Action Committee Review
```

### Appeals and Review Process

#### Internal Appeals
- Any committee member may request reconsideration
- New evidence or changed circumstances
- Procedural violations in original decision
- 30-day window for appeal submission

#### External Appeals
- Citizen complaints about AI decisions
- Community organization concerns
- Legal challenges to committee decisions
- Government official interventions

#### Review Mechanisms
- Quarterly decision review meetings
- Annual process effectiveness assessment
- External audit of decision-making processes
- Continuous improvement implementation

### Documentation Requirements

#### Decision Records
- Unique decision identifier
- Date and participants
- Problem statement and context
- Analysis and consultation summary
- Final decision and rationale
- Implementation plan and timeline
- Success metrics and monitoring plan

#### Audit Trail
- All supporting documents and communications
- Consultation records and feedback
- Committee meeting minutes and votes
- Implementation progress reports
- Post-implementation evaluation results

### Training and Competency

#### Required Training for Decision-Makers
- AI ethics principles and frameworks
- Risk assessment methodologies
- Stakeholder consultation techniques
- Legal and regulatory requirements
- Documentation and audit trail management

#### Ongoing Development
- Annual ethics training updates
- Case study reviews and lessons learned
- Best practice sharing with other jurisdictions
- Professional development opportunities

### Quality Assurance

#### Decision Quality Metrics
- Adherence to ethical principles
- Stakeholder satisfaction
- Decision outcome effectiveness
- Process efficiency and timeliness
- Appeal and reversal rates

#### Continuous Improvement
- Regular process review and refinement
- Feedback incorporation from stakeholders
- Best practice adoption from other organizations
- Technology and methodology updates

### Emergency Procedures

#### Emergency Decision Authority
- Committee Chair: Immediate authority for critical decisions
- Emergency Response Team: Technical implementation authority
- Government Executive: Override authority for exceptional circumstances

#### Emergency Decision Criteria
- Immediate threat to citizen rights or safety
- System security breach or failure
- Legal or regulatory compliance emergency
- Significant bias or discrimination discovered

#### Post-Emergency Review
- Full committee review within 48 hours
- Decision validation or correction
- Process improvement recommendations
- Communication to affected stakeholders

### Governance Integration

#### Alignment with Government Processes
- Integration with existing approval workflows
- Compliance with government decision-making requirements
- Coordination with legal and procurement processes
- Alignment with budget and resource allocation

#### Reporting and Accountability
- Regular reporting to government executives
- Public transparency reporting
- Integration with government audit processes
- Performance measurement and reporting
