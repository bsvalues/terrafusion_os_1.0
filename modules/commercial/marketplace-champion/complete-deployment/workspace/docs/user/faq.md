# Terrafusion Platform - Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### What is Terrafusion?

Terrafusion is a comprehensive government operations platform that streamlines
services, improves efficiency, and enhances citizen engagement through advanced
technology including AI, edge computing, and quantum capabilities.

### Who can use Terrafusion?

Terrafusion is designed for:

- Government employees at all levels (federal, state, local)
- Authorized contractors and partners
- Citizens accessing public services (through public portals)

### Is Terrafusion secure?

Yes, Terrafusion implements multiple security layers:

- End-to-end encryption
- Multi-factor authentication
- Role-based access control
- Regular security audits
- Compliance with FedRAMP, StateRAMP, and SOC 2

### What browsers are supported?

- Chrome 90+ (Recommended)
- Firefox 88+
- Safari 14+
- Microsoft Edge 90+

## Account & Access

### How do I get an account?

Accounts are created by your organization's administrator. You'll receive an
invitation email with instructions to set up your account.

### I forgot my password. What should I do?

1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login page
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for reset instructions
4. Create a new password following security requirements
5. If you don't receive an email, contact your administrator

### How do I change my password?

1. Log in to Terrafusion
2. Go to Settings > Security
3. Click "Change Password"
4. Enter your current password
5. Enter and confirm your new password
6. Click "Update Password"

### What is two-factor authentication (2FA)?

2FA adds an extra security layer by requiring:

1. Something you know (password)
2. Something you have (phone/authenticator app)

This prevents unauthorized access even if your password is compromised.

### Can I use Terrafusion on my mobile device?

Yes! Terrafusion offers:

- Responsive web interface for mobile browsers
- Native iOS app (iPhone/iPad)
- Native Android app
- Offline capabilities for selected features

## Technical Issues

### Terrafusion is running slowly. What can I do?

Try these steps:

1. Clear browser cache and cookies
2. Check your internet connection
3. Close unnecessary browser tabs
4. Disable browser extensions
5. Try a different browser
6. Contact support if issues persist

### I'm getting an error message. What should I do?

1. Take a screenshot of the error
2. Note the error code (if displayed)
3. Try refreshing the page
4. Log out and back in
5. Contact support with error details

### Why can't I see certain features?

Features may be hidden due to:

- Role-based permissions
- Department restrictions
- Feature flags/beta access
- License limitations

Contact your administrator for access requests.

### How do I clear my browser cache?

**Chrome:**

1. Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete (Cmd+Shift+Delete on Mac)
2. Select time range
3. Check "Cached images and files"
4. Click "Clear data"

**Firefox:**

1. Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete (Cmd+Shift+Delete on Mac)
2. Select time range
3. Check "Cache"
4. Click "Clear Now"
