## Performance Management and KPIs

### Financial Performance Framework

#### Comprehensive Financial Metrics

```
Financial Performance Dashboard:

Profitability Metrics:
├── Gross Margin: Overall and product-line gross margin analysis
├── EBITDA Margin: Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization
├── Operating Margin: Operating income as percentage of revenue
├── Net Margin: Net income as percentage of revenue
├── Return on Assets (ROA): Net income divided by total assets
├── Return on Equity (ROE): Net income divided by shareholders' equity
├── Return on Invested Capital (ROIC): Operating profit divided by invested capital
└── Economic Value Added (EVA): Economic profit after capital costs

Growth and Efficiency Metrics:
├── Revenue Growth: Year-over-year revenue growth rate
├── Recurring Revenue Growth: Annual recurring revenue growth
├── Customer Acquisition Growth: New customer acquisition rate
├── Market Share Growth: Market share expansion tracking
├── Productivity Metrics: Revenue per employee and efficiency measures
├── Asset Utilization: Asset turnover and utilization ratios
├── Working Capital Efficiency: Working capital turnover and optimization
└── Cash Conversion Cycle: Days to convert investment to cash

Liquidity and Capital Metrics:
├── Current Ratio: Current assets divided by current liabilities
├── Quick Ratio: Quick assets divided by current liabilities
├── Cash Ratio: Cash and equivalents divided by current liabilities
├── Debt-to-Equity Ratio: Total debt divided by shareholders' equity
├── Interest Coverage Ratio: EBIT divided by interest expense
├── Debt Service Coverage: Operating cash flow divided by debt service
├── Free Cash Flow: Operating cash flow minus capital expenditures
└── Cash Flow Yield: Free cash flow divided by enterprise value

Customer and Market Metrics:
```
