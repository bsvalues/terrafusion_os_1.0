Section 2.1 Classes of Members. The corporation shall have the following classes of members:

(c) Individual Members: Researchers, academics, and government professionals
(d) Corporate Members: Technology companies and government contractors

Section 2.2 Member Rights. Members shall have the right to:
(a) Receive notice of and attend annual meetings
(b) Vote on major corporate decisions and board elections
(c) Access research findings and educational materials
(d) Participate in Institute programs and activities

Section 2.3 Member Obligations. Members shall:
(a) Support the mission and purposes of the Institute
(b) Pay annual membership dues as established by the Board
(c) Comply with Institute policies and code of conduct
(d) Contribute to Institute activities and programs as able

ARTICLE III - BOARD OF DIRECTORS

Section 3.1 General Powers. The affairs of the corporation shall be managed by its Board of Directors.

Section 3.2 Number and Qualifications. The Board shall consist of not less than three (3) nor more than fifteen (15) directors. Directors shall have demonstrated expertise in:
(a) Artificial intelligence and technology research
(b) Government operations and public administration
(c) Academic research and educational programs

(e) Legal and regulatory affairs

(a) Research Committee: Oversight of research programs and grants
(b) Education Committee: Educational program development and delivery
(c) Finance Committee: Financial oversight and grant management
(d) Governance Committee: Board governance and policy development

ARTICLE IV - OFFICERS

Section 4.1 Officers. The officers shall be:
(a) Executive Director: Chief executive and administrative officer
(b) Research Director: Scientific research program leadership
(c) Education Director: Educational program leadership
(d) Secretary: Corporate records and governance
(e) Treasurer: Financial management and reporting

Section 4.2 Executive Director. William Spencer shall serve as the initial Executive Director, responsible for:
(a) Overall management and strategic leadership
(b) Grant acquisition and fundraising activities
(c) External relationships and partnerships
(d) Board reporting and organizational development

ARTICLE V - FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT

Section 5.1 Fiscal Year. The fiscal year shall end on December 31.

Section 5.2 Financial Controls. The corporation shall maintain:
(a) Segregated accounts for restricted and unrestricted funds
(b) Annual independent audit by certified public accountant
(c) Monthly financial statements and board reporting
(d) Grant compliance and reporting procedures

Section 5.3 Conflict of Interest. No director or officer shall have any interest, financial or otherwise, in any contract or transaction with the corporation without full disclosure and board approval.

ARTICLE VI - AMENDMENTS

Section 6.1 Bylaw Amendments. These bylaws may be amended by two-thirds vote of the Board of Directors, provided that notice is given at least 30 days in advance.
