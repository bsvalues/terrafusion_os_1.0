## 💰 **TAX STRUCTURE AND BENEFITS**

### **Partnership Taxation Benefits**

**Pass-Through Taxation Structure:**

- No entity-level federal income tax
- Income, losses, deductions pass through to members
- Avoids corporate double taxation
- Flexible profit and loss allocation opportunities
- Eligible for Section 199A QBI deduction

**Tax Planning Advantages:**

- Basis step-up opportunities for IP assets
- Flexible distribution timing for tax optimization
- Estate planning benefits through membership interest transfers
- International tax planning flexibility
- Research and development credit pass-through

**Annual Tax Compliance Requirements:**

- Form 1065 (Partnership Tax Return) - Due March 15
- Schedule K-1 issued to members
- State partnership returns as required
- Quarterly estimated tax payments (member level)

### **Intellectual Property Tax Strategy**

**IP Licensing Structure:**

- Holdings LLC owns all intellectual property
- Licenses IP to operating subsidiaries at market rates
- Creates tax-efficient inter-company income stream
- Centralizes IP ownership for protection and management

**Transfer Pricing Compliance:**

- Market rate licensing fees (8-15% of subsidiary revenue)
- Economic substance and business purpose documentation
- Comparable transaction analysis and support
- Annual transfer pricing study and documentation

**International IP Planning:**

- Delaware LLC structure supports foreign expansion
- Check-the-box elections for international subsidiaries
- Treaty benefit optimization for foreign licensing
- GILTI and Subpart F income planning
