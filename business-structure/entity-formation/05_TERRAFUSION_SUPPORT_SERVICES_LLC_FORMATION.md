# TerraFusion Support Services LLC - Entity Formation Package

## Entity Overview

**Entity Name**: TerraFusion Support Services LLC  
**Entity Type**: Delaware Limited Liability Company  
**Tax Election**: Partnership (Pass-Through)  
**Formation Date**: 2025  
**Primary Purpose**: Support services, consulting, and administrative
operations  
**Business Classification**: Professional Services (NAICS 541600)

## Strategic Purpose

TerraFusion Support Services LLC serves as the specialized support services
entity within the TerraFusion ecosystem, providing:

1. **Administrative Services**: HR, payroll, accounting, and operational support
2. **Consulting Services**: Government AI implementation consulting and training
3. **Maintenance Services**: System maintenance, support, and customer service
4. **Professional Services**: Legal coordination, compliance, and regulatory
   support

## Entity Structure

### Ownership Structure

- **TerraFusion Holdings LLC**: 100% ownership
- **Management**: Designated managers appointed by Holdings LLC
- **Operational Control**: Support Services management team

### Member Information

- **Member Address**: 108 W 13th Street, Wilmington, DE 19801

### 2. Operating Agreement

**OPERATING AGREEMENT OF TERRAFUSION SUPPORT SERVICES LLC**

**Article I - Formation and Organization**

1.1 **Formation**: The Company has been formed as a Delaware limited liability
company pursuant to the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act.

1.2 **Name**: The name of the Company is "TerraFusion Support Services LLC."

1.3 **Registered Office**: The registered office of the Company in Delaware is
located at 108 W 13th Street, Wilmington, Delaware 19801.

1.4 **Term**: The Company shall continue in perpetuity unless dissolved in
accordance with this Agreement or Delaware law.

**Article II - Purpose and Powers**

2.1 **Purpose**: The Company is organized for the purpose of:

- Providing administrative and support services to TerraFusion ecosystem
  entities
- Offering professional consulting services for government AI implementations
- Managing customer support, maintenance, and training operations
- Coordinating legal, compliance, and regulatory support functions

  2.2 **Powers**: The Company has all powers necessary or convenient to carry
  out its purpose.

**Article III - Members and Membership Interests**

3.1 **Initial Member**: TerraFusion Holdings LLC is the sole initial Member.

3.2 **Membership Interest**: The initial Member holds 100% of the membership
interests.

3.3 **Capital Contribution**: The initial capital contribution is $50,000.

**Article IV - Management**

4.1 **Manager-Managed**: The Company is manager-managed.

4.2 **Appointment of Managers**: Managers are appointed by TerraFusion Holdings
LLC.

4.3 **Authority**: Managers have full authority to manage Company operations
within the scope of the Company's purpose.

**Article V - Allocations and Distributions**

5.1 **Profit and Loss Allocation**: All profits and losses are allocated 100% to
TerraFusion Holdings LLC.

5.2 **Distributions**: Distributions are made at the discretion of the Manager
to TerraFusion Holdings LLC.

## Tax Strategy

### Partnership Tax Treatment

- **Pass-Through Entity**: No corporate-level taxation
- **Schedule K-1 Reporting**: Income/losses flow through to Holdings LLC
- **QBI Eligibility**: Support services qualify for Section 199A deduction

### Tax Optimization Benefits

1. **Administrative Expense Allocation**: Centralized cost management
2. **Professional Services Income**: QBI-eligible service revenue
3. **Equipment Depreciation**: Technology and equipment write-offs
4. **Training and Development**: Deductible employee education expenses
