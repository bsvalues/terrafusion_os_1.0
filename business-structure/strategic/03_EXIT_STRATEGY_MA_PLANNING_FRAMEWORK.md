# TerraFusion Exit Strategy & M&A Planning Framework

## Executive Summary

This comprehensive framework outlines TerraFusion's strategic approach to exit
planning and mergers & acquisitions, positioning the company for maximum value
realization through various strategic alternatives including IPO, strategic
acquisition, private equity, and management buyout options.

**Enterprise Valuation Target**: $8-25B enterprise value by year 7-10  
**Primary Exit Strategy**: Strategic acquisition by major technology or
government contractor  
**Alternative Strategies**: IPO, private equity growth capital, management
buyout  
**Value Realization Timeline**: 7-10 years from inception

**Strategic Objectives**:

- Maximize shareholder value through strategic exit options
- Build sustainable competitive advantages and market position
- Create multiple exit pathways and strategic alternatives
- Optimize tax efficiency and value distribution
