### **Insider Trading and Securities Compliance**

```
INSIDER TRADING COMPLIANCE POLICY
════════════════════════════════

Covered Persons:
□ Directors and officers of all entities
□ Employees with access to material information
□ Consultants and advisors with inside information
□ Family members and associated persons
□ Temporary insiders (lawyers, accountants, investment bankers)

Prohibited Activities:
□ Trading on material nonpublic information
□ Tipping others about material nonpublic information
□ Trading during blackout periods
□ Short selling or derivatives trading
□ Hedging transactions in company securities

Material Information:
□ Financial results and projections
□ Major contracts and business developments
□ Acquisitions, mergers, and strategic transactions
□ Management changes and strategic initiatives
□ Regulatory approvals and government actions

Trading Restrictions:
Blackout Periods: Quarterly earnings and major events
Pre-Clearance: Required for directors and officers
Trading Windows: Designated periods for permitted trading
Holding Requirements: Minimum holding periods for equity
Reporting Requirements: SEC and internal reporting

Compliance Procedures:
□ Pre-clearance procedures for stock transactions
□ Quarterly certifications from covered persons
□ Trading monitoring and surveillance systems
□ Regular training and education programs
□ Violation reporting and investigation procedures

Enforcement:
Violations: Subject to disciplinary action up to termination
Civil Penalties: SEC enforcement and monetary penalties
Criminal Penalties: Federal prosecution for willful violations
Disgorgement: Return of trading profits from violations
```
