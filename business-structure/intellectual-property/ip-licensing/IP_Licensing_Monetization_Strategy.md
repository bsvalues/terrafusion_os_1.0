## 💼 **IP LICENSING BUSINESS MODELS**

### **📋 LICENSING STRUCTURE FRAMEWORKS**

**1. Exclusive Technology Licensing**

```
EXCLUSIVE LICENSING MODEL
═══════════════════════

Structure and Terms:
• Exclusive rights to licensed technology in specific field or geographic area
• High initial license fees with ongoing royalty payments
• Performance milestones and minimum revenue guarantees
• Technology transfer and training support included
• Field restrictions and non-compete provisions

Typical Agreement Terms:
Initial License Fee: $2,000,000-10,000,000
Annual Minimum Guarantee: $500,000-2,000,000
Royalty Rate: 5-12% of net product revenue
License Term: 7-15 years with renewal options
Field Restrictions: Specific market verticals or geographic limitations

Exclusive Licensing Targets:
• Major defense contractors for defense-specific applications
• Government software companies for specific market segments
• International partners for geographic market expansion
• Strategic partners requiring competitive advantages

Revenue Model Benefits:
✓ High initial license fees providing immediate revenue
✓ Predictable minimum revenue guarantees
✓ Long-term royalty streams from successful products
✓ Strategic partnership and market validation benefits
```

**2. Non-Exclusive Technology Licensing**

```
NON-EXCLUSIVE LICENSING MODEL
═══════════════════════════

Structure and Terms:
• Non-exclusive rights enabling multiple licensees in same field
• Lower initial fees with broader market penetration
• Standardized licensing terms and conditions
• Technology support and updates included
• Volume-based pricing and tiered royalty structures

Typical Agreement Terms:
Initial License Fee: $500,000-3,000,000
Annual Maintenance Fee: $100,000-500,000
Royalty Rate: 3-8% of net product revenue
License Term: 5-10 years with automatic renewal
Market Restrictions: Minimal restrictions for broad adoption

Non-Exclusive Licensing Targets:
• Systems integrators and consulting companies
• Regional and smaller government contractors
• International technology companies
• Academic and research institutions

Revenue Model Benefits:
✓ Multiple revenue streams from various licensees
✓ Broad market penetration and technology adoption
✓ Lower risk through diversified licensee base
✓ Market standard establishment and technology validation
```
