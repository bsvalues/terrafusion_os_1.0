- **Role**: Commercial marketplace operations and private sector sales
- **Daily Operations**: Commercial customer acquisition, platform operations

**TerraFusion Institute** (501c3 - Research and Education)

  content

### Inter-Entity Coordination Procedures

#### 1. Daily Coordination Protocol

**Time**: 8:00 AM EST Daily Standup

- **Participants**: Entity leads, C-suite executives
- **Format**: 15-minute virtual meeting
- **Agenda**: Operational updates, resource coordination, issue escalation
- **Documentation**: Shared daily report dashboard

#### 2. Weekly Strategic Alignment

**Time**: Fridays 2:00 PM EST

- **Participants**: All entity management teams
- **Duration**: 60 minutes
- **Focus**: Strategic initiatives, resource allocation, performance review
- **Deliverables**: Weekly strategic alignment report

#### 3. Monthly Financial Consolidation

**Timeline**: Within 10 business days of month-end

- **Process**: Entity-level financial reporting → Holdings consolidation
- **Review**: CFO review → CEO approval → Board presentation
- **Output**: Consolidated financial statements and variance analysis

#### 4. Quarterly Strategic Planning

**Schedule**: Month following each quarter end

- **Duration**: 2-day strategic planning session
- **Participants**: All entity leaders, key department heads
- **Deliverables**: Updated strategic plan, resource allocation, performance
  targets

### Resource Sharing Framework

#### Shared Services Model

1. **Human Resources**: Centralized through Support Services LLC
2. **Legal and Compliance**: Coordinated through Holdings LLC
3. **Finance and Accounting**: Hybrid model with entity-specific and shared
   functions
4. **IT Infrastructure**: Shared technology platform with entity-specific
   customizations
5. **Facilities**: Shared office spaces with allocated cost centers

#### Transfer Pricing and Inter-Company Agreements

- **IP Licensing**: Holdings LLC → Operating entities (5-8% royalty rates)
- **Management Services**: Support Services → All entities (cost-plus 15%)
- **Technology Services**: OS LLC → Other entities (fair market value)
- **Administrative Services**: Allocated based on headcount and revenue
