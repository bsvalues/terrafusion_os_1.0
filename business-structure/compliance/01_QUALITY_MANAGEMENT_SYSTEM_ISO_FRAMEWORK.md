# TerraFusion Quality Management System (QMS) - ISO 9001:2015 Framework

## Executive Summary

This comprehensive Quality Management System establishes TerraFusion's
commitment to delivering exceptional government AI technology solutions through
systematic quality management, continuous improvement, and ISO 9001:2015
compliance. The QMS framework ensures consistent quality delivery, customer
satisfaction excellence, and sustainable competitive advantage.

**Quality Policy**: TerraFusion is committed to delivering world-class
government AI solutions that exceed customer expectations, comply with all
regulatory requirements, and drive continuous improvement across all
organizational processes.

**Quality Objectives**:

- Achieve >95% customer satisfaction scores consistently
- Maintain <1% defect rate in product delivery and services
- Deliver >98% on-time project completion rate
- Achieve zero compliance violations or audit findings
- Realize 10% annual improvement in key performance indicators
