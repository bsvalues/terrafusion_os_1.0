## 📝 **GRANT WRITING PROCEDURES AND STANDARDS**

### **1. Grant Development Process**

#### **Standard Operating Procedure for Grant Applications**
```yaml
Grant Development Timeline (12-16 weeks):

Week 1-2: Opportunity Assessment and Go/No-Go Decision
Procedure:
  □ Detailed RFP/solicitation analysis and requirements review
  □ Technical alignment assessment with TerraFusion capabilities
  □ Competitive landscape analysis and differentiation strategy
  □ Resource requirement analysis and budget estimation
  □ Strategic value assessment and portfolio fit evaluation
  □ Go/No-Go decision by Grant Review Committee

Decision Criteria:
  - Technical Alignment: >80% match with TerraFusion expertise
  - Competitive Position: Top 3 potential awardees assessment
  - Resource Availability: Adequate personnel and infrastructure
  - Strategic Value: Alignment with organizational objectives
  - Success Probability: >25% estimated success rate

Week 3-4: Team Assembly and Partnership Development
Procedure:
  □ Principal Investigator assignment and commitment
  □ Technical team identification and role assignment
  □ Partner institution outreach and collaboration agreements
  □ Advisory board engagement and letter of support requests
  □ External consultant identification and engagement agreements

Team Composition Requirements:
  - Principal Investigator: PhD with relevant expertise and track record
  - Technical Team: Appropriate expertise coverage for all technical areas
  - Partners: Complementary capabilities and established relationships
  - Advisory Board: National/international experts in relevant domains

Week 5-8: Technical Content Development
Procedure:
  □ Literature review and state-of-the-art analysis
  □ Technical approach development and methodology design
  □ Preliminary results compilation and feasibility demonstration
  □ Innovation articulation and competitive differentiation
  □ Risk assessment and mitigation strategy development

Quality Standards:
  - Technical Accuracy: All technical claims verified and supported
  - Innovation: Clear advancement beyond current state-of-the-art
  - Feasibility: Demonstrated through preliminary results or analysis
  - Methodology: Rigorous and appropriate for research objectives
  - Risk Management: Comprehensive identification and mitigation

Week 9-12: Proposal Writing and Assembly
Procedure:
  □ Technical narrative development following template guidelines
  □ Budget development and justification preparation
  □ Timeline and milestone development with realistic scheduling
  □ Management plan and team coordination structure
  □ Evaluation and dissemination planning

Writing Standards:
  - Clarity: Clear, concise, and accessible technical writing
  - Structure: Logical flow and organization following template
  - Evidence: All claims supported by data, citations, or analysis
  - Compliance: Full adherence to funding agency requirements
  - Professional: Publication-quality writing and presentation

Week 13-16: Review, Refinement, and Submission
Procedure:
  □ Internal technical review by subject matter experts
  □ External expert review and feedback incorporation
  □ Editorial review for writing quality and clarity
  □ Compliance verification and formatting check
  □ Final assembly, quality assurance, and submission

Review Process:
  - Technical Review: Domain experts evaluate technical merit
  - Management Review: Project management and feasibility assessment
  - Editorial Review: Writing quality and communication effectiveness
  - Compliance Review: Requirements adherence and formatting
  - Final Review: Grant committee approval and submission authorization
```

### **3. Review and Approval Process**

#### **Grant Review Committee Structure**
```yaml
Grant Review Committee Composition:
Chair: Chief Technology Officer
Members:
  - Principal Investigator (applicant)
  - Technical Director (domain expertise)
  - Financial Director (budget and compliance)
  - External Expert (independent technical review)
  - Grant Writing Director (writing and presentation)

Review Criteria and Scoring:
Technical Merit (40 points):
  - Innovation and novelty (10 points)
  - Technical approach and methodology (10 points)
  - Feasibility and preliminary results (10 points)
  - Risk assessment and mitigation (10 points)

Project Management (25 points):
  - Team qualifications and expertise (10 points)
  - Timeline and milestones (8 points)
  - Budget and resource allocation (7 points)

Impact and Significance (25 points):
  - Scientific and technical impact (10 points)
  - Broader impacts and benefits (8 points)
  - Commercialization and sustainability (7 points)

Presentation and Compliance (10 points):
  - Writing quality and clarity (5 points)
  - Compliance and formatting (5 points)

Scoring Scale:
  - Excellent (9-10 points): Exceptional quality, minimal improvements needed
  - Very Good (7-8 points): High quality, minor improvements recommended
  - Good (5-6 points): Acceptable quality, moderate improvements needed
  - Fair (3-4 points): Below expectations, major improvements required
  - Poor (1-2 points): Inadequate quality, fundamental revisions needed

Approval Thresholds:
  - Immediate Approval: >85 total score (85-100 range)
  - Conditional Approval: 70-84 total score (revisions required)
  - Major Revision Required: 50-69 total score (significant improvements needed)
  - Rejection: <50 total score (fundamental issues, not ready for submission)

Review Process Timeline:
  - Initial Review: 5 business days for committee member review
  - Committee Meeting: 2-hour review meeting with all members
  - Feedback Compilation: 2 business days for written feedback
  - Revision Period: 1-2 weeks for revisions based on feedback
  - Final Approval: 2 business days for final review and approval
```
