## 🎯 OPTION C: LICENSE-BACK MODEL
### "Harris Uses Marketplace, Can Buy Later"

### What Harris Gets
✅ **Full usage rights** to marketplace engine and modules  
✅ **Lower upfront cost** - annual fee vs. $20M-$40M purchase  
✅ **Try-before-buy flexibility** - prove value before committing  
✅ **Conversion option** - can buy outright anytime (credit 50% of fees paid)  
✅ **Budget predictability** - annual expense vs. large capital outlay  

### What William Keeps
- Marketplace IP ownership
- Ongoing relationship through annual renewals
- Ability to license to non-competing markets
- Upside if Harris doesn't convert to purchase

### Financial Terms
**Annual License Fee:** $2,000,000 - $5,000,000 per year (renewable)

**Conversion Option:**
- Harris can buy outright for $20M-$40M at any time
- Credit 50% of annual fees already paid toward purchase price
- Example: After 3 years at $3M/year ($9M paid), purchase price = $35M - $4.5M = $30.5M

**Total Package:**
- cOS Infrastructure: $10M-$20M (upfront)
- Marketplace License: $2M-$5M/year (ongoing)
- Royalty: 3-5% on cOS-attributable revenue (10 years)
- William's EVP role: $5M mission bonus

### Harris's Benefits
- **Lower initial capital** - $2M-$5M/year vs. $20M-$40M upfront
- **Flexibility** - can cancel if marketplace doesn't deliver value
- **Try-before-buy** - prove county demand for modules before purchasing
- **Partial credit** - fees paid reduce eventual purchase price

### Harris's Risks
- **Higher long-term cost** - if Harris uses for 5+ years, license fees exceed purchase price
- **Ongoing dependency** - William retains control, not full Harris ownership
- **Competitive risk** - William could license to others (though ROFR provides some protection)
- **Budget uncertainty** - annual expense vs. one-time capital investment

### Best For Harris If:
- CFO has annual budget flexibility but not large capital approval
- Harris wants to test marketplace demand with counties first
- Board is risk-averse and prefers "try before buy" approach
- Harris plans to decide on full purchase within 2-3 years
