## 📦 THE OFFER (Minutes 12-20)

### Present the Three-Layer Structure

**"Let me show you how this actually works financially:"**

#### Layer 1: Upfront Security ($10M-$20M)
**"Harris buys the TerraFusion cOS IP rights. This recognizes the years of work I've put in, and gives me immediate security. For Harris, it's less than what you've already spent on failed internal attempts."**

#### Layer 2: Long-Term Revenue Share (3-5% royalties)
**"I don't want a lump sum and walk away. I want to grow with Harris. So you pay me 3-5% of cOS-powered revenue for 10 years. Step-down bands mean as you scale, my percentage drops. We're completely aligned - I only win when Harris wins."**

**Example:** "If Harris adds $100M in cOS-powered revenue, I make $5M that year. If it grows to $400M, I make ~$12M. But my rate drops as you scale, so it's always fair to Harris."

#### Layer 3: Future Exit Option (Put/Call at 5 years)
**"After 5 years, if this is a massive success, Harris can buy out my royalty stream for a set multiple - say 10-15x. Or if Harris hits certain KPIs, I can force the buyout. That gives both of us an exit path with no surprises."**

**Example:** "If I'm making $10M/year in royalties at Year 5, Harris can buy me out for $100M-$150M and own it completely. Or I can force that if Harris has 500+ counties on cOS and $200M+ uplift."

### The Role Component

**"Here's where I fit in:"**

**"I join Harris as EVP or Chief Platform Architect. I'm not running P&L or managing dev teams - I'm doing three things:"**

1. **"Evangelizing to counties"** - assessor-to-assessor credibility in sales
2. **"Guiding platform strategy"** - helping Harris products integrate with cOS
3. **"Bridging customer needs and tech"** - I understand both sides better than anyone

**"3-5 year contract, with a $5M bonus when Harris hits 1,000 counties on cOS. Then we both reassess."**

### Why This Structure Wins

**For Harris:**
- ✅ Immediate platform ownership
- ✅ Aligned incentives (royalty only pays if Harris succeeds)
- ✅ Future optionality (buyout at scale if strategic)
- ✅ Sales leverage (William's assessor credibility)
- ✅ No operational risk (source code escrow, step-in rights)

**For William:**
- ✅ Immediate security ($10M-$20M upfront)
- ✅ Long-term wealth (royalties scale with Harris success)
- ✅ Mega-exit potential ($100M-$200M buyout)
- ✅ Career impact without operational burden
- ✅ Legacy (TerraFusion becomes government standard)

## 🧠 PSYCHOLOGICAL TACTICS

### Make Him the Hero

**Throughout the conversation, position the EVP as the visionary:**

"You saw this 7 years ago when we first talked about co-developing. You were right then, and this is the proof."

**Why:** Leaders want to be right. If this is "his idea" that you're completing, he'll champion it internally.

### Use "We" Language

**Instead of:** "I built TerraFusion..."  
**Say:** "We can launch the Harris AI Platform..."

**Instead of:** "You should buy this..."  
**Say:** "We can structure this so Harris wins and I'm aligned..."

**Why:** Partnership language reduces threat and builds collaboration.

### Anchor on His Pain

**Keep referencing Harris's specific pain points:**
- "The 3 dev team reorgs"
- "10+ fragmented systems"
- "35% margins vs. 60% potential"
- "Tyler and Esri convergence threat"

**Why:** People buy solutions to pain, not features. Keep the pain visible.

### Show Vulnerability

**Be honest about what you want:**

"I'm not good at running companies. I'm good at building platforms. That's why I want to work for Harris - you handle the business, I handle the architecture."

**Why:** Vulnerability builds trust. Shows you're not a threat, you're human.
